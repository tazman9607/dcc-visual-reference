## Special page — Mordecai
- Book 2 form: Incubus, Level 50, guildmaster / non-combatant NPC.
- Well-built, young and extremely handsome male; skin is dusky grey.
- Short devil horns from forehead; long grey-and-black hair in a ponytail.
- Barbed tail; folded black bat-like wings.
- Initially wears a tuxedo with jacket slits for the wings; he quickly removes the jacket and tie and opens the shirt because he hates formal wear.
- Voice is noticeably deeper in this form.

**Page direction:** Do not make a normal text-heavy dossier. Create the Mordecai Forms Archive as a visual-first special page: large MORDECAI identity title, compact recurring-forms subtitle, then large form panels. For the Book 2 panel, depict the Incubus form faithfully. Minimal text: BOOK II, INCUBUS, and only a tiny identifying note if required.

**Do not invent:** No lore essay, no generic demon redesign, no forcing Book 1 facial anatomy onto an explicitly different body, no later-book form information.

## New Book II visual briefs

### Katia Grim
- Blonde-haired woman using a difficult Doppelganger race; class is Monster Truck Driver.
- At introduction her attempted human form is visibly imperfect: eyes too big and too high, crooked sideways nose, lumpy chin, individual hairs too thick.
- Her speech/mouth movement looks slightly wrong, like overdubbing.
- She appears to wear a poorly sewn tracksuit and blocky snow boots; equipped gear can be absorbed into her reshaped mass.
- She is 'like clay' and manually sculpts her own body and clothing; reshaping hurts.
- With practice and Mordecai's help her face becomes much more natural by later Book 2, though still visibly rough / imperfect.
- She is shy and timid in manner, and becomes a core member of Carl and Donut's party.

**Generation direction:** Use the later-Book-2 improved human mimic as the hero image, while right-side studies can show the imperfect early face and clay-like reshaping concept. Keep blonde hair, tracksuit/snow-boot visual language, and subtle uncanny imperfections. She should read as a real person struggling to sculpt a human form, not a rubber monster.

**Do not invent:** Do not make her conventionally flawless. Do not use later-book transformed forms. Do not invent armour or a signature weapon not supported by Book 2.

### Hekla
- Icelandic crawler and leader of Brynhild's Daughters.
- Book 2 race: Amazonian; class: Shieldmaiden.
- After transformation she is about a foot taller than before and much wider.
- Pretty blonde woman who was already fit; now resembles a professional bodybuilder.
- Clear strength build with substantial dexterity.
- Uses an automatic magical crossbow, described as tearing targets up like a ranged chainsaw.
- Later references note her striking sapphire-blue eyes.

**Generation direction:** Hero should emphasize the Amazonian Shieldmaiden transformation: very tall, broad, heavily muscled blonde woman, powerful but recognizably human, carrying the automatic magical crossbow. Nordic influence is acceptable only when it supports the explicit Shieldmaiden framing; avoid costume-cliche overload.

**Do not invent:** No tiny-waisted fantasy pin-up proportions, no melee weapon replacing the crossbow, no later-book equipment.

### Lucia Mar
- Young crawler whose Book 2 race becomes Lajabless; class later shown as Black Inquisitor General.
- The transformation changes her from a young street-kid appearance into a full-grown adult woman.
- Her face alternates between a great beauty and a skull-faced monstrosity.
- She spends roughly half the day in each state; magic-focused beautiful form versus strength-focused skull form.
- She now walks with a limp.
- Her two rottweilers remain dogs.

**Generation direction:** Use a deliberately dual-form dossier: one adult beautiful form and one skull-faced monstrous form of the same person, with matching pose/silhouette where possible. The transition itself can be explored in studies. Include the limp subtly in stance. The rottweilers may appear only as secondary supporting context, not as the main subject.

**Do not invent:** Do not depict her as a child in the Book 2 hero state. Do not invent demon horns/wings or later-book forms.

### Tsarina Signet
- Explicitly an Elite NPC, not a crawler.
- Half Naiad, Half High-Elf Summoner; Level 60.
- About five and a half feet tall; extra-thin humanoid build.
- Long pointed elf ears; short horns from forehead; sharpened fangs.
- Skin gives off a mild luminescence.
- Covered from toes to face in thick-lined old-school tattoos of monsters, especially Asian and nautical imagery: oni, Chinese dragons, sharks, octopuses.
- The tattoos are alive and visibly move across her body.
- At first encounter she is effectively naked except for a thong; visually intimidating and glowing.

**Generation direction:** The living tattoos are the defining feature. Hero image should show a lean, luminous half-elf/half-naiad woman with short horns, pointed ears, fangs, and animated old-school monster tattoos flowing over her body. Frame/crop or styling should preserve modest presentation while remaining source-faithful.

**Do not invent:** Do not classify her as a crawler. Do not turn tattoos into static decorative sleeves. No later-book costume or faction details.

### Miss Quill
- Elderly female Skyfowl, Level 30, initially described as Assistant to the Magistrate.
- Feathers are tinged grey.
- Large beak is cracked and crazed like old brittle pottery.
- Sour, suspicious expression; dedicated gatekeeper personality.
- Uses a perch rather than a chair behind an office desk.
- Her desk/reception area is cluttered with papers, sushi, and a huge collection of small tagged stuffed creatures / Beanie-Baby-like collectibles.

**Generation direction:** Show an elderly avian bureaucrat on her perch behind a cluttered reception desk. Grey-tinged feathers and the cracked old beak are non-negotiable. Her obsessive collectible display is excellent environmental storytelling and should appear in the hero scene or studies.

**Do not invent:** Do not make her owl-like unless source morphology supports it; keep her as a Skyfowl. Do not soften her into a kindly grandmother archetype.

### Quint
- Desperado Club pharmacist; Level 75; Half-Djinn, Half-Garbage Scowl; non-combatant NPC.
- Small, floating drug dealer.
- Top half resembles a sharp-toothed, beady-eyed opossum.
- Bottom half is a whirling miniature tornado / dervish, constantly twisting and creating visible wind.
- Unexpectedly deep, growly voice.

**Generation direction:** Hero should make the hybrid anatomy immediately readable: nasty opossum-like upper body floating over a compact spinning cyclone lower body, inside the smoky neon Desperado Club. Keep him small rather than human-sized.

**Do not invent:** No genie legs, lamp, robes, or generic Arabian-djinn imagery unless directly supported. The tornado body is the signature.

### Pustule
- Female Hobgoblin, Level 30, proprietor of Hobgobs and Boom Sticks.
- About six feet tall; large and muscular.
- Looks like a larger, muscular goblin with an intentionally ugly/unsettling face.
- Open sore on cheek bubbles with black and green ooze; pus runs down face and shoulder.
- Wears a threadbare pink graphic T-shirt featuring a unicorn wearing sunglasses.
- Smells of rot and sickness; voice is surprisingly feminine.
- Shop tables carry dynamite, Hob-lobbers, smoke curtains and other explosives.

**Generation direction:** Lean into the contrast: intimidating six-foot muscular hobgoblin explosives merchant wearing the absurd faded pink unicorn shirt. The bubbling black-green cheek sore is a crucial identifier. Use the explosives shop as context, not random fantasy armour.

**Do not invent:** No cute goblin treatment, no armour replacing the shirt, no excessive gore obscuring the readable character design.

### Remex
- Important correction: Remex is NOT a boss and NOT a lich.
- Book 2 reveals him as Remex - Soul Leech Capacitor, Level 1, Bereft Minion of Miss Quill.
- Looks much like Featherfall in general Skyfowl body shape, but zombified and featherless.
- Eyes are black swirling orbs.
- Hazy black smoke rises from his body.
- Ethereal worm-like wisps swirl around him like a parody of the full wings he once had.
- A thin golden thread of light enters his chest and tethers him to a golf-ball-sized amber soul crystal floating in the room.
- He is frightened, huddled, gasping and in visible agony rather than behaving like a triumphant necromancer.

**Generation direction:** Depict the revealed truth, not the presumed lich: featherless zombified Skyfowl, black swirling eyes, smoke and spectral worm-wings, physically tethered by golden soul-light to the amber crystal. His pose should feel trapped and terrified.

**Do not invent:** Absolutely no lich robes, crown, staff, necromancer throne, or boss treatment.

### Krasue
- Level 16 ghost-class monsters.
- Undead shell of women; nocturnal blood-feeders.
- True combat form is a flying female head with organs trailing from the open neck.
- Loose intestines, lungs and other viscera hang beneath on wet tendrils of nerve/artery.
- They twist and corkscrew through the night sky and howl/scream like banshees.
- They can hide their true form by leaving the rest of the corpse elsewhere; killing the flying head/organs is not enough to fully kill them.
- Only injured by magic or magical items.

**Generation direction:** Make the flying-head-and-organs anatomy unmistakable and frightening. A grouped night scene with multiple Krasue is appropriate, supported by anatomical study panels explaining the separated body concept visually without overloading with text.

**Do not invent:** No generic vampire woman with a full body. No ghost-sheet aesthetic. No sanitising away the hanging organs.

### Circus Monsters
- Grimaldi's Traveling Circus is populated by transformed descendants/performers with a decayed red-and-black circus aesthetic.
- Former Circus Lemurs: red-furred lemurs wearing black-and-red painted human skulls as masks; throw/juggle knives.
- Stilt clowns appear throughout the encampment and help erect barricades.
- Groups of short, fat clowns are present.
- Giraffes move through the ruins, sometimes laden with lemurs.
- A large ogre-like freakshow brute wears a leopard-skin leotard and has a tentacle sprouting from the side of his neck.
- The circus contains tents, cages, wagons, freakshow signage, bone/wood barricades and trench-style defences.

**Generation direction:** Use a grouped bestiary spread rather than separate pages. Hero scene should feel like one coherent corrupted circus ecosystem. Give the lemurs, stilt clowns, short clowns, giraffe and freakshow brute readable sub-studies on the right.

**Do not invent:** Do not invent a single canonical 'circus monster' species. Keep the group visually varied and tied to the source-described circus.

### Heather the Bear
- Level 19 Neighborhood Boss.
- Former roller-skating circus bear; one of Grimaldi's longest-running attractions.
- Old/tired bear transformed into a monstrosity, barely recognizable from her former self.
- Overwhelming speed and strength; hunts prey and drags victims back to the circus.
- Later imagery around the fight includes pale/white parasitic worms associated with her transformed state.

**Generation direction:** Preserve enough roller-skating circus-bear identity to make the tragedy legible, but heavily corrupt the old attraction into a powerful, diseased monstrosity. Motion should communicate frightening speed rather than a stationary zoo bear.

**Do not invent:** No cute performing-bear presentation. Do not invent unrelated armour or humanoid anatomy.

### Ringmaster Grimaldi
- Ringmaster Grimaldi - Pestiferous Vine; Elite Level 85 City Boss.
- Before transformation, Redstone Grimaldi was a dwarf ringmaster associated with red-and-black coattails and top hat in signage.
- Current boss form is enormous: initially mistaken for part of the tent or a stage prop.
- More like a giant bush/shrub than a simple vine; occupies the entire centre ring and reaches the tent ceiling, swallowing the centre pole.
- Pale green; multiple python-like roots spread across the ground.
- Collective mind-control / regenerative role over the circus, associated with spores and parasitic worms.

**Generation direction:** Hero image should sell scale: the Pale green Pestiferous Vine filling the centre ring under a circus tent, roots spreading into bleachers, with tiny performers for comparison. Right studies can include the old dwarf-ringmaster sign as historical context, but the boss form is the primary subject.

**Do not invent:** Do not depict current Grimaldi as a humanoid dwarf in ringmaster clothes. Do not reduce the vine to a man with tendrils.
